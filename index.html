FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

A typical small-business site takes two to four weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on the number of pages and how quickly the content is ready.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. We can build on an easy-to-edit platform and show you how to change text, images and pages without touching any code.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

We can set up hosting, connect your domain and look after renewals, or work with the provider you already use.

What do I need to get started?

Just a short brief: what your business does, who your customers are and a few websites you like. We'll guide you through the rest.
